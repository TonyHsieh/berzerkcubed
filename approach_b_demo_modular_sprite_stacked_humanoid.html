How Approach B Works

Instead of baking 340+ full-body slices, the trooper is split into 3 independent voxel stacks rendered bottom-to-top:

1. Lower Rig (Legs & Pelvis) 6 Layers

Rotates toward movement velocity. Cycles through 4 gait frames when running, or tucks knees when airborne.

2. Upper Rig (Torso & Helmet) 10 Layers

Mounted on top of the pelvis Z-height. Rotates 360° independently toward the mouse cursor with subtle breathing/stride bob.

3. Weapon Rig (Pulse Rifle) 4 Layers

Parented to the torso's shoulder socket. Kicks backward along the aim vector during recoil without needing extra body slices.
